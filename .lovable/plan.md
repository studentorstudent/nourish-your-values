

# GoodFood — Healthy Nutrition & Personal Values SLC Website

A modern, responsive single-page website inspired by your existing Tilda template, rebuilt with better design and easier content management.

## 1. Homepage / Hero Section
- Full-screen hero with a vibrant food photography background and dark overlay
- **"GoodFood"** branding at top
- Headline: **"Healthy Nutrition Starts With Our Values"**
- Tagline: *"Discipline in nutrition creates freedom in life"*
- Mission statement (2–3 sentences about reshaping how people think about food)
- CTA button: **"Follow Our Weekly Progress"** that scrolls to the blog section
- Smooth scroll navigation bar (Home, About, Team, Progress)

## 2. About the Project Section
- Clean section explaining what SLC (Student-Led Contribution) is
- Why healthy nutrition matters for students — focus, energy, long-term success
- **Values cards** showcasing the 5 core values with icons:
  - Responsibility, Discipline, Awareness, Self-respect, Community Impact
- Warm, motivational tone throughout

## 3. Team Section
- Grid of team member cards with photos/avatars and roles:
  - Miras Zhanabayev — Text Editor
  - Assel — PR Manager
  - Alinur — Project Coordinator
  - Mukhammedin — Researcher
- Clean card design with hover effects

## 4. Weekly Progress Section (Blog Style)
- Card-based layout showing weekly challenge posts
- Each card shows: date, title, short description
- Cards link to dedicated detail pages for each challenge/update
- Easy to add new weekly entries by editing a simple data file
- Initially populated with your existing entries (Challenge 1, Challenge 2 — Values and Goal)

## 5. Footer
- Inspirational quote: *"Sustainable nutrition begins where intention meets structure"*
- Contact email and copyright
- Back-to-top button

## Design & Style
- Dark theme with warm accent colors (greens, oranges) matching the food/nutrition theme
- High-quality food imagery backgrounds (using free stock photo URLs)
- Smooth scroll animations between sections
- Fully responsive — looks great on mobile and desktop
- Modern typography with clean spacing
